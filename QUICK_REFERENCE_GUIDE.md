## ❓ **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

### Q: Is 135 L/day realistic for India?
**A**: It's the official urban standard, but many use less. Rural is 55-75L. The app helps you find your sweet spot (target: 80-100L direct).

### Q: Does my score only include direct water?
**A**: No! It combines direct (what you see) + virtual (food, clothes, energy, digital). This is your TOTAL impact.

### Q: Can I change India's total footprint?
**A**: You individually? No. But collectively, Indians can by:
- Eating less meat (reduces 92% agricultural use)
- Buying less (clothing water)
- Using renewable energy (reduces power plant cooling)

### Q: Should I feel guilty about high score?
**A**: No! This is awareness, not judgment. You NOW know where your water goes. You can choose to change.

### Q: How often should I retake the survey?
**A**: Monthly is good. Set one habit change per month, then see improvement next month.

### Q: Is bucket bathing really that much better?
**A**: Yes! 18L vs 90L for shower = 72L saved per day = 26,000L per year per person = can supply 6 months drinking water for 1 person!

### Q: What about RO purifier water waste?
**A**: It's real. A typical RO purifier wastes 3-4L to make 1L clean water. Add this to your score.
