## 🇮🇳 **INDIA-SPECIFIC BENCHMARKS**

### **National Standards**
Per Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs:
- **Urban domestic standard**: **135 L/capita/day** (lpcd)
- **Rural minimum delivery**: **55 L/capita/day** (Jal Jeevan Mission)
- **Essential needs (WHO minimum)**: **50-100 L/day**

### **Current Reality**
- Only ~30% of India's population has access to safe drinking water
- **India's total water footprint**: 1,182 cubic km/year (largest globally with China & US)
- **India's blue (freshwater) footprint**: 243 cu.km (24% of global total)
- **Domestic water use**: ~4% of India's total footprint
- **Agricultural use**: ~92% of total (rice, wheat, sugarcane, cotton irrigation)

### **Water Availability Crisis**
- **4% of world's fresh water** but **18% of world's population**
- Ranks **120 out of 122** nations for water quality
- Ranks **133 out of 180** nations for water availability
- **2.9 billion liters/day** of untreated wastewater dumped (especially Ganga)
- **Overexploited groundwater** in 41% of cereal-growing regions

### **Recommended Usage Breakdown (for 135L)**
- Toilet flushing: **45 L** (33%)

- Laundry: **40 L** (30%)

### **Sustainable Target for India**

- **Combined impact**: Realistic reduction to **1,000-1,500 L/day** total

*"India must reduce water footprint while ensuring food security for 1.4 billion people"*

## 🔬 **METHODOLOGY & SOURCES**

### **Water Footprint Calculation**
Based on Hoekstra & Chapagain (2004) framework:

**Total Water Footprint = Green + Blue + Grey Water**

- **Green water**: Rainwater (from agriculture, not human-caused scarcity)
- **Blue water**: Surface & groundwater (directly consumed, often causing scarcity)
- **Grey water**: Freshwater needed to dilute pollutants to safe levels

### **Direct vs Virtual Water**
- **Direct use** (20-30% of total): What you see—showers, toilets, washing
- **Virtual/embedded** (70-80% of total): Hidden in food, clothes, energy, products

This app focuses on both for complete picture.

### **Scoring Methodology**
- **1-4**: In the green (efficient)
- **4-8**: Average to high (room to improve)
- **8-10**: Excessive (urgent action needed)

Score combines direct + lifestyle (virtual) water use.

### **Key Academic Sources**
1. **Hoekstra, A.Y. & Chapagain, A.K.** - Water Footprint Network foundational work
   - Citation: Hoekstra & Chapagain (2004) - Water footprint of nations
   
2. **Mekonnen & Hoekstra (2011)** - Green, blue, grey water footprints of crops
   - Citation: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences
   
3. **BIS IS 1172-1993** - Indian Standard for water consumption norms
   - Bureau of Indian Standards (Official government standard)
   - Specifies 135 L/day for urban domestic use
   
4. **Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs** - National Building Code guidelines
   - Government of India (Official policy document)
   - Jal Jeevan Mission standards: 55-75 L/day rural
   
5. **Water Consumption in Residential Buildings** - IJCRT Publication
   - Study on India's domestic water usage patterns
   - Confirms 135 lpcd urban, 75 lpcd rural
   
6. **WHO & FAO Guidelines** - Minimum water requirements for health
   - WHO minimum: 50-100 L/day for basic needs
   
7. **World Bank & UN Water** - Global water availability studies
   - India water security assessments
   - Availability and accessibility data
